01

Introduction

Min Wan ("we," "us," or "our") operates the LegatoScore mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Summary. LegatoScore is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not operate any servers that receive your data, we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and nothing you record, scan, or create in the App is transmitted anywhere. All data stays on your iPad unless you choose to share or export it yourself.

02

Information the App Accesses

  • Microphone. LegatoScore uses your iPad's microphone to listen to your live performance and to record reference performances you choose to create. This audio is processed and/or stored only on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Camera. LegatoScore uses your iPad's camera to let you scan sheet music into PDF files for use within the App. Scanned images/PDFs are stored only on your device.
03

How Data Is Stored

Reference recordings and their associated analysis data, along with any scanned scores, are saved locally in the App's storage on your iPad. This data remains on your device until you delete it yourself within the App (or delete the App itself). We do not have access to this data, and it is not backed up to any server or cloud service operated by us.

04

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of the following through the App: your name, email address, contact information, location, browsing history, or any device identifiers. The App makes no network requests and does not share data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics provider, because it has no server-side component at all.

05

Third-Party Services and SDKs

LegatoScore does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only diagnostic information that may exist is anonymous crash data that Apple itself may collect through the operating system's standard crash-reporting mechanism (this is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not ours, and we are not responsible for disclosing data collected directly by Apple).

06

Your Choices and Data Deletion

Because everything is stored locally, you are always in control: you can delete any individual recording or scanned score within the App at any time, and deleting the App itself will remove all associated local data from your device.

07

Children's Privacy

LegatoScore does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all. The App is not directed specifically at children, but its design (an on-device music practice tool) means no personal data is transmitted regardless of the user's age.

08

Future Changes

LegatoScore may add features in future updates — including user accounts, iCloud sync, and subscriptions — that would change how data is handled (for example, by transmitting data to a server for the first time). If and when that happens, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features become active, and the App's App Store Connect privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.
