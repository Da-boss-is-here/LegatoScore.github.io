Frequently asked questions

8 topics
Does LegatoScore need an internet connection?

No. LegatoScore works entirely offline — every feature, including listening to your playing and turning pages, runs without Wi-Fi or cellular data.

Why does the app ask for microphone access?

LegatoScore needs your microphone to listen to your reference recording and to follow your live playing so it can turn pages automatically. Audio is processed on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

Why does the app ask for camera access?

The camera is used to scan your physical sheet music into a PDF you can use inside the app. Scanned pages stay on your device.

Is my data ever uploaded anywhere?

No. LegatoScore currently has no servers and makes no network connections. Everything — recordings, scans, and analysis data — stays on your iPad. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app isn't turning pages at the right moment — what should I try?

Try re-recording your reference performance in a quiet room, at a consistent volume, and mark your page-turn points at natural pauses or phrase breaks rather than mid-passage.

Does LegatoScore support iPhone?

Not currently — LegatoScore is designed and optimized for iPad only.

Will accounts, subscriptions, or iCloud sync be added?

These are planned for a future update. The current version works fully offline with no account required.
